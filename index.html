About Landr

We land the plane.

Landr builds empires. We see what’s coming before the market does, build the brand that owns it, and land it with the buyers who matter: at scale, at handback or at exit.

Our story

Two operators. One question.

LDR-001 → LDR-007

What if the next great brand didn’t have to wait for permission?

Between them, Trent Butler and Stephen Brandt have spent 50 years creating, managing and scaling global brands. They have founded 8-figure start-ups and scaled businesses into 9 figures. Steve has built and run large global companies. Trent is a founder, disruptive thinker and the author of Fractology.

They watched the same thing happen again and again. Big companies spotted the opportunity, then lost it to process, politics and approval gates. Manufacturers built brilliant products for other people’s brands. Great ideas launched without a plan for who would one day buy them.

Then AI arrived and the old playbook died. Scale became cheap. Direction became everything.

So they built Landr: a venture powerhouse that thinks like a startup, acts like an empire, and takes responsibility for landing the plane.

The name

The plane is built into the logo.

Plenty of people can get an idea off the ground. Our job is to land it. That’s why the plane sits inside our name, not beside it: the landing is the point.

Our ethos

Six principles.
One flight path.

How every Landr venture gets from first signal to a planned landing.

  1. 01Start at the destination.Launch, scale, handback or exit. We know where it lands before it takes off.
  2. 02Read the signals.Pattern Intelligence picks the opportunity and the route.
  3. 03Build it whole.Product, brand and commercial model, designed as one venture.
  4. 04Hold the line.Clear priorities, milestones and decision gates. Focus on what matters.
  5. 05Own the outcome.People, partners, capital and execution. We take responsibility.
  6. 06Let evidence decide.Test, learn and act. Continue, change course or stop.
Flight rules

Our flight rules.

  • The fast eat the slow.Size is no longer the advantage. Speed with direction is.
  • Patterns beat plans.Linear plans assume the future arrives in order. It doesn’t.
  • Build for the next owner.Every venture is designed so a buyer can see the value on day one.
  • Evidence over opinion.Every stage ends at a decision gate: continue, change or stop.
  • AI accelerates. Humans decide.AI compresses the work. Experienced judgement sets the destination.
  • Accountable delivery.We don’t hand over a deck. We land the plane.

Skin in the game

In the agency world, ideas are cheap when it’s someone else’s money.

We won’t recommend you spend a dollar on something we aren’t already doing and funding ourselves.

That’s why we own and run our own brands. It’s our test bed and our insurance policy. We put our own funds and ideas into practice first, learn what works and what doesn’t, then bring those lessons to our clients.

Some call owning brands a conflict. We see it as the security a client needs: proof that the people advising you have their own capital on the line.

  • We test with our money, so you don’t have to
  • Full disclosure of our own brands and investments
  • Every engagement under a confidentiality agreement
  • Category conflicts agreed in writing, up front
  • Access to capital for the right ventures (how it works)
The engine

Why nobody launches faster.

Our core IP is Pattern Intelligence: spotting trends early, then creating the brands that own them. Four parts keep the engine running.

  1. 01Map the signals

    We travel the world mapping signals and trends: expos, factories, retail and consumers across the USA, Australia and Asia. It keeps us moving ahead of industries, not behind them.

  2. 02Find the adjacent targets

    Most agencies aim for the bullseye everyone can see. We use the law of adjacency to find the targets next to it, the ones your team hasn’t considered yet.

  3. 03Test with our own funds

    We launch it ourselves, fast. Small, measurable tests with our own capital show what the market actually does, not what a focus group says it might.

  4. 04Amplify with clients

    What works, we bring to our clients, built for their strengths, their scale and their portfolio. You get the learning without paying for the mistakes.

Our day rate may look higher. Your total cost is lower. We’ve already spent our own money on the tests that failed, so every dollar of yours goes straight to what works, and gets to market faster.
